The space right of the count

Six things to put beside 0 / 2. Everything else on the card — the name, the count, the bar, the button — is exactly as it ships, so only the one box is in question.

All six are free. CrewWeek.checkIns is every check-in in the week, carrying pactId, userId and day, and members is the whole roster — so which days you kept and who else is in today are both already on the card, just never read. The one thing that is not free is a per-pact streak: streakWeeks is the crew's, not the pact's.

A

Who else is in

The crewmates who have kept this pact today, as faces, with the ones still out greyed. It is the only option that puts another person on the card — which is the whole reason the pact is in a crew and not a notes app.

Cost: no new data. Avatars are already loaded and cached for the crew strip. Reads thin in a crew of one.

Which days

Seven day cells: kept filled, today ringed, days already gone left pale. The bar under the count says how many; this says which — and answers “did I skip Monday again?”, which no count can.

Cost: no new data. But it sits directly above the segment bar saying a near-identical thing, so one of the two probably has to go.

What is left

The count from the other end. 0 / 2 is what you have done; this is what the week still wants — and it is the number that decides whether you go today.

Cost: no new data, and the least of any option. It is also the thinnest: it is arithmetic on two numbers already on the card.

Ring

A ring carrying the same fraction as the number, weighted as a shape rather than a figure. It balances the count optically and fills the box cleanly.

Cost: no new data, but it is the third time the card states one fact — number, ring, bar. Weight, not information.

E

Icon as watermark

Say nothing, but stop the space reading as a mistake: the pact's own icon, oversized and let down into the tint, bleeding off the right edge. The card gets a face per pact.

Cost: no new data. It is decoration — the box stops looking empty without becoming useful. Needs clipping, and competes with the small icon up top unless that one goes.

F

Close it up

The other answer: the box is empty because it does not need to exist. Pull the count up under the name and let the card end where its content does — the stack gets shorter and more of Home's crew panel shows above it.

Cost: no new data. Home sizes the stack to the space it has, so a shorter card means deciding what takes the space instead.
