The today strip

Partly, yes — but not all of it, and the overlap is smaller than it looks.

The strip and the pact card answer different questions. The strip's _done(member) is week.checkedToday(member.id).isNotEmpty — has this person kept anything today. The pact card's faces are per pact, and only for the card at the front of the deck. On a crew with one pact they say exactly the same thing; on a crew with four they do not, and the strip is the only place the day rolls up.

Before cutting it, note what would go with it:

5H LEFTThe day's clock. Nothing else on Home says how long is left, and it is the whole pressure behind a nudge.
The nudgeThe strip is a drawer front. Pulling it open is the only way into CrewMemberList and the only place in the app you can nudge someone.
The roll-upWho is in across every pact, not just the one showing. Four pacts means four swipes to learn the same thing from the cards.
