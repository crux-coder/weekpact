Undo check-in — ideas

Eight ways to take a check-in back. Every card is live: press Check in, then try to undo it. Reset puts a card back to checked in.

What ships today (A, _CompletedCheckIn in today_widgets.dart): a cream "Checked in today" bar with an underlined Undo at its right end. Since a063315 that link opens a confirm dialog. The ideas split into two kinds: B, C, D, E make the gesture itself hard to hit by accident, so they need no dialog. F, G, H move undo somewhere else or limit when it can be used.
