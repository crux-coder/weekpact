Check-ins without a photo, as a story

Today a pact that doesn't ask for a photo opens as the pact's colour wash with the chip in the corner: a blank frame waiting for a picture that never comes. Every direction below uses only data the app already has (pact title, icon, sorbet, kept time, this week's check-ins, crew) unless marked otherwise. Shown on the Mint pact "Run 5k".

Recommendation: start with 6 · Poster + week. The flat sorbet card tells a text check-in apart from a photo at a glance, the title carries the meaning, and the week strip gives the story a little news ("3 of 4") so it isn't the same frame every day. It needs no schema change. Later, adding the optional note (5) drops straight into the empty middle of the card. 4 · Crew chorus is the strongest social nudge, but it repeats across every member's story for the same pact, so it's better as a one-off end card than as each check-in.
