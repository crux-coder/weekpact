Pact card carousels

Eight ways the pact stack could move, each one live: drag or swipe the cards, or use the arrows. Every phone shows the bottom of Home with the streak and week cards above, so you can see how each style sits against them. The dashed yellow lines mark the content edges.

How to read these: the first two keep the card flush with everything above it, which is what you asked for. Gapped pages is the same as now with a little air between cards while swiping, so they don't look glued together. Peek and Hero say "there's more" without dots but give up the flush edge. Stacked deck is the old one. Cover flow is the most playful and the least like the rest of the app.
