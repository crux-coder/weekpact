New pact palettes

Four new hue families, built from scratch rather than from today's clay-and-sage set. Each one is drawn on the real Home pact card, with today's palette kept as a baseline. The ten colours are ordered so that pacts next to each other in a crew's list are far apart in hue.

Every option clears the floors the theme already promises: card ink ≥ 7:1, the muted caption ≥ 4.5:1 (ink at .74 over the tint) and the badge glyph ≥ 6:1. Outlines, raised edges and the checked-in edge are still mixed from each tint, as in HomeSurface. The app is locked to dark mode (ThemeMode.dark), and the tints are the same in both modes, so the cards sit on the dark canvas by default. Switch the canvas to see them on the light one.
