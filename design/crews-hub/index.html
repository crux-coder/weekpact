Crews as the hub, no Pacts tab

Pacts already belong to a crew. Today the Pacts tab and the Crews tab each carry their own crew switcher over the same crew. Here the Crews tab lists every crew you're in, and tapping one opens that crew's page, where its pacts and members are managed together. The tab bar drops to three tabs.

What changes

Now

HomePactsCrewsAccount
  1. Pacts tab → switcher → pick a crew → see or edit its pacts
  2. Crews tab → switcher → pick the crew again → members
  3. The two pages share one selected crew, but nothing on either page says so

Proposed

HomePactsCrewsAccount
  1. Crews tab → list of your crews (with their week at a glance)
  2. Tap a crew → its page: pacts, members, invite, leave
  3. Crews no longer needs a switcher; Home keeps the one it has

1 · The flow

Crews list → crew page. One page per crew, opened by a normal push, with a back chevron. The ⋯ menu holds the rare actions (rename, leave, delete) so they don't sit at the bottom of every crew page.

Open questions

One crew only: skip the list and open the crew page directly? I'd keep the list. It costs one tap, it's predictable, and it's where "Start another crew" lives.
Home → "Open pacts" link: it currently jumps to the Pacts tab. It would push the crew page for the crew Home is showing.
Who can edit pacts: if only owners can add or edit, members see the pact list read-only and the ADD control is hidden, like INVITE today.
