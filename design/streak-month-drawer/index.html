Streak card — “View month” drawer

The card already mixes two clocks: the flame counts weeks, the squircles show days this month. A drawer is the place to connect them and answer what the card can’t: which days, who was out, and what the weeks meant for the streak. Four directions below, all built only from data the crew page already loads — CrewWeekSummary.shareOut / percent per finished week, CrewWeek.checkIns (with photos and claps), members and pacts. Sample: a 4-person crew, today is Thu 22 October, streak 0.

A

Day calendar + who was out

The card’s dots, grown up: each day shaded by how much of the crew checked in (same scale as the history calendar). Tap a day and the panel below lists every member, which pacts they kept, and their photos. Missed members get a dimmed row, today shows a Nudge.

Data: shareOut per day (finished weeks) + the live week’s check-ins. Day detail needs check-ins for past weeks — today only the live week has them, so this needs a checkIns(from, to) query.

B

The month in photos

Every day that someone checked in shows the top photo (most claps) as its tile, with a count badge when there were more. Tapping opens the existing story viewer filtered to that day. Below, the month’s most-clapped check-in. The most emotional option — it makes the month feel like a scrapbook rather than a scorecard.

Data: photoUrl + clapCount on check-ins. Same past-check-ins query as A, plus signed photo URLs for ~30 images (thumbnails, ideally).

C

Who carried the month

A row per member, a tick per day (week gaps between Mon–Sun groups). Instantly answers “who keeps showing up and who drops off”. Under it, a small leaderboard by days out. Social and a little competitive — fits a crew app, but needs a soft touch so it doesn’t shame.

Data: per-member-per-day “was out” booleans for the month. Derivable from check-ins; shareOut alone is not enough (it is crew-level).

D

Why the streak is what it is

Bridges days and weeks. Stat chips on top, then the month’s weeks as rows: a lit flame for a kept week (gold-tinted), a grey one for a broken week, a dashed flame for the live week. The bottom card turns the 0 into a goal — “keep this week to start a streak” with how close you are. Answers the question a 0 flame actually raises.

Data: everything is already loaded: CrewWeekSummary for finished weeks, percentCrew + days left for the live one, streakWeeks. No new backend.
