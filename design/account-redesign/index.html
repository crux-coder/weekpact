Account, a few directions

Today Account is a settings page with a face on it. Every direction below turns it into your record: what you have kept, across every crew, plus the crews themselves, with Edit profile kept one tap from the top. They differ in what leads: numbers (1, 7), photos (2), days (3, 5), pacts (4) or tabs that split the page (6). Settings, Log out and Delete account stay in every one, either at the foot or behind a gear. Scroll inside each phone.

Where I'd start: 1 · Record is the safest step: it keeps today's profile card and settings, and slots history and crews between them, so nothing people already know moves. If you want the page to feel like a place people visit for fun, 2 · Photo wall is the strongest, because the check-in photos are the most personal thing the app holds. 6 · Tabs is the tidiest if the page is going to keep growing.

Data cost:

  • All of them need a "my check-ins across every crew" query. The backend reads check-ins per crew week today, so this is one new read-only RPC (additive, fine for older builds).
  • Lifetime counts and best streaks (1, 4, 7) can be computed in that same RPC.
  • Crews rows reuse what the Crews tab already fetches.
