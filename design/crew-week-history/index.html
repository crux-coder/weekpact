Crew week — scrolling back through history

Four ways to reach past weeks from the crew week page. Every phone is drawn on the chassis that ships today: charcoal canvas, 34pt crew name, progress + streak pair, the pact carousel with neighbours peeking, dots, the stone "checked in today" panel. Each option adds one control and leaves the rest of the page alone. In every option a past week is read-only: no today marker, no check-in, and the stone panel becomes a "that week" summary.

A

Week stepper on the subtitle

The "This week · Sep 21–27" line grows two chevrons. Tap ‹ and the whole page becomes last week; tap › to come back. The right chevron is disabled on the current week. Swiping the pact card horizontally still moves between pacts; the page never gets a second swipe axis.

Cost: one control, one date offset on fetchWeek. Risk: going back eight weeks is eight taps. That is fine: history here is "last week and the one before", not a year.

B

Week strip

A horizontal row of week chips between the title and the progress pair, newest on the right. Each chip carries the crew's percentage and a thin bar, so the history is legible before you tap anything. Tap a chip to load that week below; the strip scrolls further back.

Cost: one extra call for the summaries (a `crew_week_summaries` view: week_start, percent). Risk: it is a second row of numbers above the progress card that already says a number. On short phones the pact card loses ~70pt.

C

Past weeks stack below

The page stops fitting one viewport and simply scrolls. This week stays exactly as it is; under the stone panel a "Past weeks" list runs back in time, one row per week: the dates, seven day-segments, the crew percentage. Tap a row and it opens as the full page in the past-week state (as in A).

Cost: the page's FittedBox / one-viewport contract goes, which is a real refactor of `crew_week_page.dart`. Gain: the most discoverable: you cannot miss history if it is under your thumb. Pairs naturally with A for the detail view.

D

Pull the card down for last week

The pact card gains a second axis. Left/right is still pacts; drag the card down and the same pact's previous week slides in from above, its edge already peeking as a ghost strip. The card wears a small week tag so you always know when you are. The header and the progress pair follow the card's week.

Cost: a nested PageView, and the vertical drag fights pull-to-refresh, which the page has today. Gain: the most direct: "the same pact, one week ago" is one gesture with no chrome added. Best as an addition to A, never alone: a hidden gesture needs a visible control somewhere.
