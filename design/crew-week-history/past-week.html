Crew week — telling a past week apart

What shipped opens a finished week on the same page as the live one, and the two look alike: same crew name, same progress pair, same card, only the small subtitle and the bottom panel differ. Five ways to make the past week read as the past at a glance. A + B together is the recommendation: the title changes shape, and the instrument panel becomes a closed-week banner, with no new colour language and no new gesture.

A

The date is the title

A past week leads with when. The big headline becomes the date range, the crew name drops to a line under it, and a grey "PAST WEEK" chip sits above. The live page keeps its shape (crew name big, "this week" small), so the two are never the same silhouette.

Cost: none beyond copy and two text styles. Gain: the difference is in the first thing you read.

B

Closed-week banner

The progress + streak pair is the live week's instrument panel; a finished week does not need a dial. It becomes one wide banner: the percentage, "Week closed", who hit their target, and a lock. The streak card and the "checked in today" panel go. Less on the page, and none of it looks like a thing to act on.

Cost: one new banner widget. Combine with A. Gain: the read-only feel comes from the layout, not from a label.

C

Archive tone

A finished week is drawn as grey paper. The pact card loses its tint and takes the neutral inset grey, empty day cells go flat, the badge goes grey, a small "CLOSED" mark sits in the corner. Kept days stay green, so the record is still legible. Colour means live; grey means archive.

Cost: a second colour language for the card, which the design system says not to add. Risk: a grey card reads as disabled or loading, and a crew's pact colours are how you tell pacts apart.

D

A sheet over this week

A past week never becomes "the page". It rises as a sheet over the live week, which stays dimmed behind it; a grab handle and an X close it. The title bar carries the date and "Last week", and the crew name is not repeated because it is visible behind. You cannot mistake it for the live page because the live page is still there.

Cost: the sheet has to scroll the same tall card, and full-height sheets fight the carousel's horizontal swipe less than you would think but the vertical dismiss can catch. Gain: strongest sense of place.

E

Week tabs under the name

A row of week tabs under the crew name shows where you are in time: earlier weeks to the left, "THIS WEEK" on the right in the streak's orange. The selected tab is underlined. Swiping the page sideways moves between weeks; the pact carousel keeps its own swipe inside the card. The live tab is the only warm-coloured one, so a past week reads as "not the orange one".

Cost: the tabs and the page's swipe compete with the carousel's. This makes the past-weeks list under the page redundant, so it is really a different feature. Gain: history and position in one control.
